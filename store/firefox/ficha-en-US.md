## Summary (max. 250 characters)

```
Fills in usernames, passwords and two-factor codes from your sOC Credentials vault, with no server: it talks to the app on your PC.
```

## Description

```
**sOC Credentials** is a password manager with no server: your vault lives encrypted on your PC (and, if you want, in your own Google Drive or OneDrive). This extension connects it to Firefox.

**What it does**
- When you enter a username or password field, it shows the matching entries for that site right below and fills them in with one click.
- Gives you the site's two-factor code (TOTP), ready to copy or fill in.
- Offers to save what you type when you sign in to a new site.
- Password generator in the toolbar button.
- If you want, it turns off Firefox's password manager so you are not offered both at once.

**How it works**
The extension stores nothing and talks to no server: it asks the **sOC Credentials** app on your PC for the entries through native messaging. While the vault is closed it only tells you to open it.

**Requires** the sOC Credentials app for Windows (free, open source): https://github.com/donki/Credentials/releases

No ads, no telemetry, no accounts of ours.
```

## Privacy policy

```
sOC Credentials collects no data. The extension stores nothing and sends nothing to the internet: it only talks, inside your own computer, to the sOC Credentials app through native messaging. The vault is kept encrypted on your computer or, if you choose, in the app's private folder of your own Google Drive or OneDrive. No server of our own, no telemetry, no third parties.
```
